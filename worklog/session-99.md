## Gotchas / learnings

- **Subagent wait-loops don't survive agent stops:** the w5rw
  implementer twice stopped "waiting for a monitor" on a Pkg.test child
  it could no longer see; the orphaned julia process kept running with
  its stdout pipe lost. Orchestrator took over the gate (waited for pid
  exit, ran its own suite). Pattern: when a subagent says "waiting for
  the monitor", check `ps` — the process may be orphaned; re-run the
  gate yourself rather than resuming the wait.
- **Stale completed-agent notifications recur** (same task-id fires on
  wait-loop cleanup) — ignore once the work is committed.
- **bd tracker healthy this session** — no jsonl re-import needed
  (first time in 3 sessions); `bd dep add` worked throughout.
- **3+1 convergence held again** on both blind rounds (vanm, addq) —
  same attractor-state signal as session 98.
